Grape Seed Extract

2

Green Tea Extract

Green tea extract contains catechins, including EGCG, which have antioxidant properties. These compounds are studied for their potential effects on cellular health. The evidence does not establish that green tea extract can restore hearing or eliminate ear ringing.
3

Gymnema Sylvestre

Gymnema sylvestre is a plant traditionally used in herbal preparations, particularly those associated with glucose metabolism. Its presence in ZenCortex contributes to the product's botanical profile, but reliable evidence supporting a direct benefit for hearing health is lacking.
4

Capsicum Annuum

Capsicum annuum is the plant species that includes many varieties of chili pepper. It contains capsaicinoids and other naturally occurring compounds. These substances have been studied for several biological effects, but their inclusion in a supplement does not demonstrate improved hearing.
5

Panax Ginseng

Panax ginseng is a traditional medicinal plant containing compounds called ginsenosides. Researchers have investigated its potential effects on cognitive performance and other aspects of health. There is insufficient evidence to conclude that it improves hearing or treats tinnitus.
6

Astragalus

Astragalus is a botanical ingredient used in traditional herbal practices. Its roots contain various plant compounds that have been studied for their biological properties. Research has not established that astragalus supplements improve auditory function or reverse hearing loss.
7

Chromium Picolinate

Chromium picolinate is a form of the trace mineral chromium. Chromium is involved in normal nutrient metabolism, although the benefits of supplementation depend on individual circumstances. The ZenCortex label lists chromium separately from its proprietary herbal blend.
8

Maca Root

Maca is a plant native to the Andes and is commonly used in dietary supplements. It has been studied for its potential effects on energy, mood, and general well-being. There is not enough reliable evidence to establish a specific benefit for hearing or tinnitus.
